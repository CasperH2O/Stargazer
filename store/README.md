# Stargazer

Stargazer turns Jibo into a window on the universe. He shows an astronomy picture from ESA/Hubble, ESA/Webb or NOIRLab, with its name, where it is in the sky and who took it.

Stargazer needs BEam 3.2.0 or newer and an internet connection. It asks for no special access to Jibo.

![Stargazer on Jibo: a Webb picture with its caption, the settings, and a Hubble picture](banner.png)

## Using it

Once installed, **Stargazer** is a button in Jibo's main menu. You can also say "Hey Jibo" and then:

- "Stargazer", "open Stargazer", "start Stargazer", "launch Stargazer"
- "show me the stars", "show me the night sky", "show me the universe", "show me space"
- "look at the stars", "let me see the stars", "I want to see the stars"
- "go stargazing", "let's go stargazing"

Say just those words, nothing more. It needs Jibo to hear you through his cloud.

When you open it, a telescope eyepiece looks at the horizon first, with grass along the bottom of the lens. Then it rises towards the sky and looks around, with Jibo's own telescope sounds. Its focus dial lights up as the picture loads. Then the picture comes into focus inside the lens, and the lens opens up to the whole screen.

The caption has three lines:

- **Title:** the picture's name. Jibo reads it aloud.
- **What and where:** for example M 30 · Capricornus · Hubble.
- **Credit:** who took the picture.

After 10 seconds, Jibo's eye comes back. Touching the picture restarts that time.

- **Tap the picture:** shows the title, the source and date, the description and the credit. A long description comes in pages: swipe sideways to turn them. The gear in the top right corner opens **Settings**.
- **Swipe down:** goes back one level. On the picture, it closes Stargazer.

Each time, Stargazer picks one source at random and shows its newest picture. It prefers a source that has something you haven't seen yet, and only contacts that one source.

## Settings

Tap the picture, then the gear in the corner.

### Sources

Turn each source on or off. At least one stays on.

- **ESA/Hubble Picture of the Month:** a new picture every month.
- **ESA/Webb Picture of the Month:** a new picture every month.
- **NOIRLab Image of the Week:** a new picture every week.

### Effects

How a new picture appears.

- **Ken Burns** (on): a slow pan and zoom across the picture. Tall pictures are panned from top to bottom.
- **Telescope iris** (on): the lens opens up into the whole picture.
- **Look up** (on): Jibo tilts his head towards the sky as the eyepiece rises.
- **Starfield** (off): stars stream out before the picture, then sparkle by the title.

### More

- **Show for:** how long the picture stays before Jibo's eye returns: 10 s, 30 s, 1 min, 5 min, or Until I close it.
- **Slideshow:** off, or a picture from another source every 1, 5 or 30 minutes. A running slideshow keeps Stargazer open.
- **Read title aloud:** on or off.
- **Read description aloud:** off by default. Jibo reads the picture's description after the title.
- **Telescope sounds:** Jibo's tripod clicks as the eyepiece moves. They come from Jibo's own sound library.
- **Show caption:** on or off.

## Pictures and credits

Every picture belongs to the people and organisations named in its caption. Pictures from ESA/Hubble, ESA/Webb and NOIRLab are shared under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Stargazer shows each picture on Jibo with its credit. It does not copy, store or share pictures.

## Privacy

- **Memory only:** pictures are downloaded into memory and dropped once they leave the screen. They are never saved to disk.
- **What is stored:** only your settings, a short list of pictures already shown (for the "New" mark), and the newest picture last shown from each source (to pick a source with something new).
- **No special access:** Stargazer does not use Jibo's microphone or camera, your home network, or other programs and files. It only reaches the public picture servers.
- **No account and no tracking:** Stargazer only asks the picture sources for their latest pictures.
